## Validation qualifications

Recorded phase checkpoints include Godot 4.7.2 editor import/parsing and bounded headless/Forward+ startup validation. Automated integration tests use actual engine physics for movement, shots and navigation, with setup-specific placement where needed to isolate a transition. Passing tests do not imply every encounter configuration or hardware target has been covered.

Some parallel Phase 3 flare/recall runs emitted two-instance ObjectDB shutdown cleanup warnings. Separate affected reruns passed without those warnings. The warning was documented rather than claimed permanently fixed.

## Approved decisions worth retaining

- Ledger quantities and transfers are authoritative; neither Raiders nor world loot meshes independently create material stock.
- Mission outcome policy is separate from accounting. Original salvage stock does not block completion after Raiders resolve; ground cargo permits recovery until collected or the deadline.
- Preserve the original single-Raider behavior as a regression scenario.
- Player recall can succeed while hostiles remain and through nonlethal incoming fire. Equipment looting has its own interruption rules and is not the same channel.
- Components are allocated before deployment, then transferred on machine destruction. They use existing material accounting and recovery.
- Stage I is an isolated movement experiment, not a commitment to chassis progression, new attacks or baseline movement changes.
- Blender is available according to the director; detailed models and animations have not yet been implemented under these stages.
