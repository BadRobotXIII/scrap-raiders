Five passive normal deployments exercised allied reports, machines, scavenging and extraction, but no Raider-versus-Raider damage or betrayal. A separate fixture reproduces a concrete eligibility defect: the destroyed player remains eligible for an existing allied report until its three-second expiry.

No gameplay, configuration or automated-test source was changed during this audit. All **216 files** in the S delivery source manifest still match their recorded hashes. Existing uncommitted accepted work remains intact. No staging, commit or publication was performed. ## Evidence boundaries

In the matrices, **implemented** means the code exists and is connected; **enabled** means the normal Asset Recovery configuration activates it; **normal exercised** means an observed event occurred in the unchanged normal scenario. **Fixture verified** means an automated test or deliberately arranged condition exercised assertions. This does not prove natural frequency or human readability. **Manual evidence** refers to recorded director feedback, not agent simulations.

The director accepted P and its physical combat follow-ups, Q, R and S. Their [P](stage_p_dynamic_encounters.md), [combat follow-up](stage_p_combat_stability_followup.md), [Q](stage_q_personality_combat.md), [R](stage_r_integrated_polish.md) and [S](stage_s_mission_structure.md) reports establish historical scope and acceptance. The feedback does not separately attest every social chain in today's normal configuration. Stage T manual acceptance remains pending.

## Startup, authority and lifecycle

The actual main scene is `asset_recovery_arena.tscn`, not the base `mission_arena.tscn`. Its inherited chain is Asset Recovery → Refined Extraction → Personality Encounters → Emergent Encounters → Mission Arena → Test Arena. | Objectives/results | `MissionObjectives`, definitions, `MissionObjectivesView` | Hook accepted ledger and defeat events before admissions. | Camera permissions | `presentation/spectator_camera.gd`, `debug/developer_spectator.gd`, `gameplay/post_death_spectator.gd` | Shared camera behavior; separate developer flight and player follow permissions. | Teardown/restart | Arena, Recall, mission rules and component `stop`/exit hooks | Resolve abandonment where required, close objectives, stop weapons/AI/timers, release camera/control ownership, seal owners and free scene. Restart retains seed/scenario/stash selection with new deployment identity, actors, reports and budgets. |
| Diagnostics/presentation | `DeploymentMetrics`, encounter inspection, HUD/audio/effects | Read accepted events/state. Metrics disabled by default; bounded history and explicit truncation. They do not close objectives, drive encounters or transfer quantities. Presentation is not authoritative. |

Ordering is deliberate: social/ledger owners and objective observers exist before initial admissions; admission finishes Combat/Social wiring before actor physics. Persistent receipt verification precedes irreversible settlement. No demonstrated duplicate gameplay owner or reason for a broad rewrite was found. Local death-report eligibility is the concrete lifecycle defect described below.

## Seeded baseline observations

Fresh normal S scene, **1× time scale**, seeds **87017–87021**, stationary vulnerable robot at Recall Dock. The harness presses Deploy, enables existing developer observation and enters restricted follow after death. It does not teleport actors, grant cargo, change relationships or choose AI objectives. Captures use isolated ignored stash paths. Five processes ran concurrently, so these data support simulation/event checks, not comparative wall-clock performance.

Actual Raider `Health.damaged` instigators were recorded, not inferred from weapon hit markers or actor names. Rifle events identify intended targets, not necessarily the body struck. Allied-report counters are **0.5 s sampled occupancy counts**, not unique report deliveries. The audit event buffer is capped at 512; these runs used 41–55 records, so it did not truncate. Existing metrics' own discarded count is zero. [Raw JSON and harness](../baselines/stage_t_audit/observe.gd.txt) accompany the summary.

All frames checked material conservation, equipment validity, live Raider cap ≤5 and response commitments ≤2. All passed. Observed peak was **two Raiders**, zero response tickets; this is not peak configured population validation. Player results remained unchanged after closure. No sample had a qualifying richer ally or betrayal assessment; no recorded Raider damage came from another Raider. Guard damage accounted for seven south / 26 north damage events.

In south, the initial allies extracted at **32.83 / 52.77 s**; authored rivals arrive **70.28 / 105.42 s**. In north, initial allies extracted **14.03 / 15.20 s**. No cross-pair hostility can express itself when those actors do not overlap. ID1 is also the sole opportunist and commonly takes six units before its ally takes three; full/richer cargo gates then preclude betrayal. This is evidence of limited passive social opportunities, not proof player intervention or finite responders can never produce them. Existing ally-report intent and subsequent component recovery demonstrate that social components are not generally disconnected.

R already rejected the globally compressed `[0, 0, 15, 25, 70]` timing trial after controlled comparisons. That candidate shortened deployments without producing Raider deaths. Do not repeat it blindly or alter shared Q resources to manufacture a social result.

## Validation performed before proposing changes

All **29 existing suites passed** in a fresh sequential run. [Exit results](../baselines/stage_t_audit/validation.json) and individual UTF-8 log copies are archived. Inspected logs contain no `SCRIPT ERROR:` or `ERROR:`. The sole warning is the known **two ObjectDB instances leaked at exit** in **`flare_loot_test`**. Earlier reports sometimes called this a legacy extraction warning; this run identifies the actual suite explicitly.

Godot **4.7.2.stable.official.ed1daf0bf** editor parsing/import passed (`--headless --path . --editor --quit`). Five bounded deployed headless simulations and the two controlled audit fixtures completed successfully. Default Forward+ graphical startup (`--quit-after 180`) passed on the RTX 4060 Laptop GPU; it opens the briefing and **does not** prove active graphical mission play. The S delivery's prior graphical Deploy→pickup→recall/results evidence remains historical, not a fresh T run. No export or cross-hardware performance validation was done.

The audit probes are archived as `.gd.txt` under the existing baselines `.gdignore`; executable copies and isolated test saves are ignored under `.godot/stage_t_audit`. The dead-report fixture intentionally captures the defect, so its successful exit must not be described as a passing fix test. Assertions inspecting its output establish reproduction, not resolution.

Approve the [focused T remediation plan](../plans/phase_7_stage_t_remediation_plan.md): fix T1, close actual S integration coverage, then evaluate the smallest justified normal-only opportunity correction using the same seeds and unchanged reference presets. Keep Q tuning, weapons, health, population budgets, accounting and world architecture intact unless another concrete defect is reproduced and reviewed.

Stage T is **not complete**, and readiness for Stage U is **not established** until those changes, full validation and the director's integrated play-test pass. The plan contains the manual acceptance matrix. No implementation approval is inferred from acceptance of Stage S or from this audit.
